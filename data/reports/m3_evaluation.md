## Limitations

- 44 participants in total (about 30 in the target group): intervals are wide, and one fold holds only a handful of target participants.
- Regime A only (new participants). The temporal regime (B) is P1.
- Labels use the frozen 1-minute Dexcom series, features native readings (ADR-013); SA-1 is registered as P2.
- Associations only: nothing here is a causal effect of any feature.
