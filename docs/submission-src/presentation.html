The problem

A CGM shows what already happened.

In prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, glucose often rises above 180 mg/dL after meals. The response depends on the person, not only the meal.
Before the outcome, a clinician still cannot ask
“What was likely to happen?”
“Why?”
“What if the meal changed?”
Real native Dexcom readings, participant CGMacros-003, 13 Mar 2020 (de-identified dates)3
Healthcare use case

Decision support for reviewing a patient's meals and glucose

Who
Clinicians
Diabetes educators, endocrinologists, primary care, reviewing CGM, meal and wearable data before or during follow-up.
For whom
Prediabetes · Type 2
Groups derived from baseline HbA1c. Healthy participants are evaluated separately.
What it gives
A per-patient Twin
Per-meal risk with uncertainty, its drivers, the learned personal response, prediction vs. reality, bounded what-if, and replay.
Deliberately out of scope
No medication, insulin, diagnosis or treatment functionality. No automated actions. Not a medical device, not clinically validated, not live monitoring.

Leakage prevention

The model never sees the answer

meal start · prediction made
✓ Allowed: everything before
pre-meal glucose (native readings) · this meal's logged macros · recent heart rate and activity · baseline labs · outcomes of earlier, closed meals
✕ Forbidden: the future
glucose after meal start · this meal's outcome · “Amount Consumed” (logged after eating) · any other participant in the test fold
TIME
L20 meals read data after their start
L3features rebuilt from history cut at meal start are identical
L4folds are participant-disjoint
L5no meal inside another usable meal's window
L6no single feature suspiciously predictive
All passed in M2 · personal prior fitted per fold on training participants only · in the Twin, appending future data never changes the state hash10

Limitations

What this prototype does not claim

Small cohortabout 30 target-group participants over ~10 days: wide confidence intervals
One evaluation regimenew participants only; temporal validation is future work
Association ≠ causationwhat-if and SHAP show what the model relies on, not what would happen
Label basisfrozen 1-minute series; interpolation effect unmeasured (SA-1)
Limited what-if leverscurrent-meal macros and pre-meal activity only; no post-meal activity, medication or insulin
Not clinicalnot a medical device, not clinically validated, not publicly deployed, not live monitoring
Honesty about limits is part of the product's credibility20
